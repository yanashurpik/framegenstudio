Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

FrameGen Studio is operated by Yana Shurpik. We help SaaS founders and marketers create AI-powered demo and ad videos. Our website is www.framegenstudio.com. Questions? Email shurpik.y@gmail.com.

What data we collect

We collect only what we need to provide our services:

  • Email address — when you sign up for a webinar, the course waitlist, or contact us directly.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, and general location (country/city) collected via Google Analytics 4.
  • Ad interaction data — anonymous events (page views, button clicks) collected via the Meta Pixel to measure and improve ad performance.
  • A randomly generated ID — stored in your browser's local storage to link your own sessions together for analytics de-duplication. It contains no personal information.

We do not collect payment information. Payments are processed by third-party providers under their own privacy policies.

How we use your data

  • To send you the webinar link or course access you requested.
  • To improve our website content and understand which pages are useful.
  • To measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns on Meta (Facebook/Instagram).
  • To respond to questions or support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with any third party except the service providers listed below.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4 — website analytics. Privacy policy.
  • Meta Pixel — ad performance measurement. Privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms — processes form submissions and delivers them to our inbox. Privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel set cookies to track visits. The random session ID is stored in local storage. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the site.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Email shurpik.y@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We keep email addresses for as long as you remain on our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email we send. Analytics data is retained for 14 months per Google Analytics default settings.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects any changes. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
